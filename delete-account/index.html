Delete your Brainigo account

Brainigo, by Digitalix · Last updated: 21 November 2026

You only have a Brainigo account if you signed in. Without one, everything Brainigo holds is on your device: clear the site’s data in your browser, or uninstall the app, and it is gone.

In the Brainigo app

  1. Open Settings. Under Your account, tap Delete account….
  2. Type delete to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

In Brainigo on the web

  1. Sign in and open Settings.
  2. Under Account & sync, choose Delete account….
  3. Type delete to confirm, then choose Delete my account.

Either way, your account is closed at once, your data is removed within minutes, and your sign-in is deleted. This cannot be undone. We send one last email to confirm, in case it was not you. Practice saved on a phone before or while signed in stays on that phone until you uninstall the app.

Without signing in

Email dan.abakah@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Brainigo account”, from the email address you signed in with. We will delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in with our authentication provider, Clerk, and your name, email address and settings
  • Every learner on the account, and their practice results, progress, practice minutes and reminders
  • Drills you saved to your account
  • Homework, lesson bookings, waitlist places, and your conversations with tutors and their messages
  • Reviews you wrote (and the tutors’ ratings are corrected)
  • If you teach: your tutor listing, your lesson times, your students’ places with you, and the reviews on your listing
  • Your notices, web push subscriptions, and emails not yet sent
  • People you blocked or who blocked you, and reports you made or that were made about you

What happens for other people

  • An upcoming lesson you booked is cancelled and the time is free for the tutor again; they are told.
  • If you teach, students with an upcoming lesson are told it is cancelled.

What is kept

  • A past lesson stays in the tutor’s own history, with the learner shown as “Deleted account” and no message.
  • The confirmation email: its contents are cleared once sent, and its record goes within 30 days.
  • If your address ever bounced or reported our email as spam, it stays on our do-not-send list.
  • Usage statistics from the web are tied to a random browser identifier, not to your account, and expire under Amplitude’s retention.
